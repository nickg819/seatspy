How it works, out of your paper notebook and into your phone

  1. Set Up Your Table

    Tap a seat to add someone from your book, a stranger or one of tonight’s players waiting in the tray. You always sit at the bottom middle, so the table reads the same way every session.

  2. Tag the Players

    Twelve player types, from Calling Station to Maniac. Not sure yet? Quiz Me asks what you have seen and ranks the likely types. Next Player moves you around the table to the next seat with no type.

  3. Exploit Their Mistakes

    How to Exploit Them says when to bluff, when to bet for value and when to back off, for that player in particular. After a hand, Log What They Did records a play or a tell, with what it means and which type it points to.

  4. Your Book in Your Pocket

    Every read stays with the player. The next time they sit down, you start where the last session left off.

Learn from the comfort of your home

Everything for studying sits under Learn on the home screen.

The advice is only useful if you know what a float or a 3-bet is. The Glossary covers more than 90 poker terms, and the terms in the advice have a dotted line under them. Tap one to see what it means.

Meet the Player Types is a card for every type, with plain words under the short names, to read before a game.

The Terms quiz deals ten cards a round. Each answer turns the card over, green when you are right and red when you miss. The questions change as you go: pick the meaning, name the term, true or false, fill the blank from a real line of advice, or type the term yourself.

The Player Types quiz also deals ten cards, each a player, a hand or a tell, and you name the type. The card turns over to show what gave it away, and the types you miss come back first.

Quiz Progress keeps count of the terms you know, shows which groups you are weakest in and runs a round on just the terms you keep missing.

Equity Calculators work out each player’s showdown equity in Hold’em and Pot-Limit Omaha, double-board bomb pots included. Give up to nine players a hand or a range, paint a range on the 13 by 13 grid, and save the ones you use. New to equity? What Is Equity? covers outs and the price of a call in six short pages, then hands you a spot to guess.

The ICM Calculator works out a deal at the end of a tournament. Type the payouts and each player’s chips, and it shows what each player gets by ICM or by chip EV.

Built for the card room

Use it before a game and between hands. The whole book lives on your phone, so it works in a back room with no signal.

Incognito Mode shows each tagged seat as a plain dot. Press and hold the table to see the types, and let go to hide them again.

When someone changes seats, hold their seat and drag it to the new one. Drop them on another player and the two swap.
