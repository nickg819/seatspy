What SeatSpy Is

SeatSpy is a note-taking and study aid for poker players. It sorts the players you face into types and suggests ways to play against each type. Those suggestions are general advice. They do not account for every hand or every player, and they do not guarantee a result. You make every decision at the table.

SeatSpy also has Equity Calculators and an ICM Calculator, which work out figures from the cards, stacks and payouts you enter. Some figures come from sampling and can move a little from one run to the next. Check any figure before you rely on it. A deal you make with other players is between you and them.
